As an Associate Product Head at MagicBricks, you will play a crucial role in leading the development and execution of innovative consumer internet product strategies to drive business growth and enhance customer experiences. Your focus on data-driven decision-making, user-centric design, and cross-functional collaboration will be key to your success.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- **Product Strategy and Development:**
  - Drive the product vision, strategy, and roadmap to enhance customer experiences and business outcomes.
  - Identify market opportunities, customer needs, and trends in the consumer internet space to create impactful solutions.

- **Team Leadership:**
  - Lead and mentor a team of at least 4 product managers, fostering a culture of innovation, collaboration, and excellence.
  - Provide clear direction, set goals, and monitor team performance to ensure project delivery aligns with business objectives.

- **Cross-functional Collaboration:**
  - Work closely with engineering, design, marketing, and data analytics teams to define and prioritize product requirements.
  - Partner with senior stakeholders to align product initiatives with overall business goals.

- **Customer Focus:**
  - Leverage data insights, customer feedback, and user research to make informed decisions and continuously improve product performance.
  - Optimize the customer journey by developing intuitive, user-friendly solutions tailored to the needs of B2C customers.

- **Performance Analysis:**
  - Define key metrics to measure product success and monitor progress against these benchmarks.
  - Utilize data analytics tools to track product performance and identify areas for improvement.

**Qualifications Required:**
- Minimum of 10 years of experience in product management with a proven track record in the B2B & B2C domain.
- Previous experience in the consumer internet industry is mandatory.
- Demonstrated ability to lead teams of 4 or more members effectively.
- Strong understanding of product management methodologies, including Agile and Scrum.
- Excellent leadership, communication, and stakeholder management skills.
- Strong problem-solving abilities and a customer-centric mindset.
- Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and manage multiple priorities.
