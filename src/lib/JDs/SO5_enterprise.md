Senior AI Product Manager, Logistics Intelligence Platform

About FreightGrid
FreightGrid is a Series B enterprise SaaS company building the operating system
for mid-market freight forwarders and 3PLs. Our platform handles shipment
visibility, customs documentation, and exception management for 200+ enterprise
customers across North America and Europe, including 4 of the top 20 global
3PLs. We've raised $48M from Tier-1 enterprise investors and are scaling our
AI capabilities to move from a system-of-record to a system-of-intelligence.

Location: New York or San Francisco. Hybrid, 3 days/week in office.

The Role
We're hiring a Senior AI Product Manager to own our AI roadmap across the
platform. You'll partner with our ML engineering team to ship AI-native
features — document extraction, exception prediction, shipment ETA models,
and agentic workflows for ops teams. This is a senior IC role reporting to
the VP Product.

What You'll Own
- The AI product roadmap end-to-end. Discovery, prioritization, shipping,
  measurement, iteration.
- Pricing and packaging for AI features. Our customers pay $80K-$400K ACV;
  AI features are increasingly the wedge in renewal conversations.
- Customer discovery with VP Operations, Director of Logistics, and Customs
  Compliance leaders at enterprise accounts. You'll be on 8-10 customer calls
  per week, often alongside our enterprise AEs.
- Working with our ML team on model selection, eval design, and ship/no-ship
  decisions. We expect you to have an opinion on prompt engineering, RAG
  architecture, and when fine-tuning is worth the cost.
- GTM enablement for AI features. You'll write the enablement decks, partner
  with marketing on positioning, and join key sales cycles when needed.

What We're Looking For
- 6+ years of product management experience, with at least 3 years shipping
  enterprise B2B SaaS products to customers paying $50K+ ACV.
- Direct experience selling into or shipping for the logistics, supply chain,
  freight, or adjacent enterprise verticals. Strong preference for candidates
  who have worked with 3PLs, freight forwarders, or large shippers.
- Hands-on experience shipping AI/ML features in production — not just
  "exposure to AI." You should be able to walk us through a specific AI
  feature you scoped, shipped, and measured.
- Experience navigating enterprise sales cycles. You understand procurement,
  legal review, security questionnaires, and the difference between a
  champion and an economic buyer.
- Comfort with ambiguity. Our roadmap shifts quarter to quarter as we learn
  from enterprise design partners.

Nice to Have
- Prior experience at a Series B-D enterprise SaaS company that scaled
  through the $10M-$50M ARR range.
- Technical background — CS degree or significant engineering experience.
  You don't need to code production systems, but you should be able to
  read code and have technical conversations with ML engineers.
- Familiarity with enterprise data platforms (Snowflake, Databricks) and
  modern AI infrastructure (LangChain, vector databases, OpenAI/Anthropic
  APIs).

Compensation
$200K-$260K base, equity, full benefits. Visa sponsorship available for
exceptional candidates.
