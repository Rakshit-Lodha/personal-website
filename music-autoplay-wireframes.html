Music autoplay · 4 approaches

How should users discover the player?

Browsers block audio.play() until a user gesture, so we need an affordance. These four wireframes compare the trade-offs — from "trust the play button" to "show an explicit hint."

Approach 04

Hint + click-anywhere starts

Pros

  • Highest probability the user hears the music
  • Hint sets expectation, so audio start isn't a surprise

Cons

  • User clicks a link → music starts → feels coupled to navigation
  • Still requires hint chrome (same cost as 03)
  • If user mutes their system, nothing happens — but they think they "did something" they can't undo

Recommendation. Ship 01 as the floor and 02 as the polish — a one-time pulse on the play button. No copy, no layout shift, no risk of surprise audio when the user is scrolling or navigating. 03 is the right move only if user testing shows the chip is genuinely missed. 04 couples audio to unrelated clicks and is the most likely to feel "off."
